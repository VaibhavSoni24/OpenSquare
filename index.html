🌸 How to Play OpenSquare

🚶 On Foot Controls

  • WASD or Arrows Walk / Move
  • Shift Anime Sprint
  • Space Jump
  • Mouse Orbit & Pan Camera (Click to look)
  • Scroll Zoom In / Out
  • E or F Enter Nearby Anime Car

🚗 Driving Controls

  • W / ↑ Accelerate Gas
  • S / ↓ Brake / Reverse
  • AD / ←→ Steer Left / Right
  • Space Handbrake / Drift!
  • H or Ctrl Car Horn (Alerts NPCs)
  • L Toggle Headlights
  • R Anime Lo-Fi Radio Stations
  • C Switch Camera Perspective
  • E or F Exit Vehicle

🌆 World & Atmosphere

  • T Cycle Day / Golden Sunset / Cyberpunk Night
  • Tab or H Toggle Controls Help
